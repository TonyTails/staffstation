Staff Station

A personal assistant app that runs on its owner's own Mac.

Staff Station is a private, single-user macOS application built by Anthony Farmakides for his own use. It reads his Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Sheets to summarize mail, add meetings to his calendar, and track business expenses. Everything it reads stays on his computer.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
