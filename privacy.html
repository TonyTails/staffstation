Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Staff Station is a personal macOS application used only by its developer, Anthony Farmakides, on his own computer. It has no other users, no servers, and no accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app accesses the owner's own Gmail (reading messages, creating drafts, sending messages the owner writes and confirms, and moving messages the owner chooses to Trash), Google Calendar (reading events and adding events to the owner's own calendar), and Google Sheets (reading one spreadsheet).

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner summaries of his own mail, add his own meetings to his own calendar, and track his own expenses. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, shared, or transferred to any other person or company. The app never sends email, deletes email, or changes calendar events without the owner's explicit action in the app.

Where the data is kept

All data stays on the owner's computer, in a folder readable only by his user account on an encrypted disk. Google sign-in tokens are stored the same way. Short excerpts of an email may be sent to an AI model, on the owner's computer or at a provider the owner has chosen, only when the owner has approved that use.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Staff Station's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the app's data folder from his computer.
